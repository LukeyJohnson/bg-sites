Following our recent update to **[Create Actions Directly From Your Inbox](https://feedback.deepspacesync.com/changelog/45550)**, we now support AI summarisation to extract important insights from emails forwarded to your **Deep Space** project!

### ⚡ Simply Forward an Email Chain to Deep Space!
* AI summarises the entire chain to provide a concise summary
* The Action gets auto-assigned to you for review

![AIGeneratedEmails.png-4700](https://storage.sleekplan.com/products/287727888/changelog/46333/inline-1e268f6919f71bb34e0b30cfdb74e308.jpg)

**Why Did We Build This?**
* Simplify the process of extracting long context information from emails
* Bring AI-enablement to more workflows to save time
