**Key Features**

See your most important numbers instantly:
- Total contracts and how many are active
- Total contract value
- How much has been claimed (with percentage)
- Number of variations submitted

**Search & Filter**

Find contracts quickly by name, region, or status. Results update as you type.
![edbe114ca1987f6fa712af8abf179dd9.png-672](https://storage.sleekplan.com/products/287727888/changelog/50687/inline-da244dc3522c323464b8c99ab27304f7.jpg)
**Detailed View**

Click any contract to see six tabs of information:

1. Details - Contract info, dates, scope of work, quick edit access
2. Orders - All purchase and variation orders with totals and status
3. Variations - Track variation requests and approval status
4. Claims - Monitor progress claims and approved amounts
5. Payment Schedules - Due dates with automatic overdue alerts
6. Invoices - Track invoices from submission to payment

**Export Anything**

Every tab has an export button – download to CSV for reports or sharing.

**Why It Helps**

✅ Save Time - Everything in one place
✅ Stay Current - Automatic updates from actual data
✅ Better Visibility - Complete financial picture at a glance
