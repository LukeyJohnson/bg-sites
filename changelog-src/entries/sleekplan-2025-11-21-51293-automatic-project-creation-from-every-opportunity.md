# Introducing Project Everywhere
Every opportunity deserves a home. We've launched a game-changing feature that automatically creates and links projects to opportunities from the moment they're born - giving your team a complete, connected view of work from initial pursuit through final completion.

## What Changes Now
**Projects Created Automatically**
When you create a new opportunity, Deep Space now instantly creates an associated project in the background. No more manual setup, no more forgotten steps, no more disconnected data.

**Seamless Lifecycle Tracking**
From the first client conversation to the final sign-off, everything stays connected. Your opportunity details, project documents, correspondence, actions, costs - they all flow together naturally because they're linked from day one.

**One Source of Truth**
No more hunting across different systems wondering "where did we track that?" Every opportunity has its project, every project has its context. Your team can see the full picture whether they're in sales, delivery, or management.

**Automatic Organization**
Projects inherit key details from their opportunities - client information, workspace settings, initial metadata. The foundation is set automatically so your team can focus on the work, not the admin.

**Built-In Context Everywhere**
Open an opportunity and see its project details. View a project and understand the opportunity that created it. Switch between perspectives seamlessly because the connection is always there, always accessible.

## Why We Built This
Construction businesses don't work in silos - but too often, their systems do.

We kept hearing the same story: "We win an opportunity, then someone has to remember to set up a project. Sometimes it happens right away, sometimes it's weeks later. Sometimes people forget entirely and suddenly we're six months in with documents scattered everywhere."

The gap between opportunity and project execution was creating real problems. Sales teams couldn't see project progress. Project teams didn't have full opportunity context. Data got duplicated or lost. Critical information lived in one system but not the other.

We built Project Everywhere to eliminate that gap completely. By automatically connecting opportunities to projects from the start, we're ensuring that nothing falls through the cracks. Your pursuit becomes your project. Your project retains its pursuit context. Everything that matters stays connected throughout the entire lifecycle.

Now your team can stop thinking about where to put information and start focusing on delivering great projects.

*Project Everywhere is now live across all Deep Space workspaces. Create an opportunity and watch your project come to life automatically.*
