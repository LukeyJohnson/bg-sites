## Overview

  - Added QR buttons next to each sub-form toggle in HSEQ Manage Sub-Forms. Click to reveal a ready-to-scan code and the direct form link.

##   Why do we have this

  - Project managers can quickly grab a form link or QR in Horizon and share it with on-site staff so they can open and submit the form on their phones
    without hunting for URLs.

##   How to use it

  - Open a form’s “Manage Sub-Forms”.
  - Click the QR icon beside a sub-form; a popover shows the QR and link.
  - Share the link or let teammates scan the QR to open the form on mobile.
