# Form Attachments Auto-Upload to Document Management

## What's New

When you submit forms with file attachments (photos, documents, PDFs), those files are now **automatically saved to your project's Document Management system**. No more manual downloading and re-uploading!

---

## The Problem We Solved

Before this feature, when team members submitted forms with attachments:

- Files were stored separately from your project documents
- You had to manually download attachments and upload them again
- Attachments weren't linked to your projects
- Finding form attachments later was difficult
- Files weren't part of your document audit trail

**This created extra work and made it hard to keep everything organized.**

---

## How It Works Now

### Simple 3-Step Process

```
1. Submit Form          2. Automatic Processing       3. Ready in Documents
   with photos    ───▶     (happens instantly)   ───▶    organized in
   or files                                              folders
```

**You don't need to do anything extra.** Just submit your forms as usual, and attachments appear in your Document Management automatically.

## Where Files Are Stored

Your attachments are organized automatically:

```
Documents
└── Forms
    └── [Form Name]
        ├── site_photo_001.jpg
        ├── inspection_report.pdf
        └── defect_image.png
```

**Folder structure:**
- All form attachments go into a **"Forms"** folder
- Each form type has its own subfolder
- Files are named clearly so you can identify them

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to change how I submit forms?
**A:** No! Just submit forms as you normally do. The upload happens automatically in the background.

### Q: How quickly are files available?
**A:** Files typically appear in Document Management within seconds of form submission.

### Q: What if I submit multiple photos?
**A:** All photos are uploaded. Each file becomes a separate document in your project.

### Q: Can I still access attachments in the form system?
**A:** Yes, attachments remain available in both places.

### Q: What happens if a file fails to upload?
**A:** The system will automatically retry. If issues persist, our support team is notified.

### Q: Are the files linked to specific projects?
**A:** Yes! Files are automatically associated with the project selected in the form.

### Q: Can I move files to different folders after upload?
**A:** Yes, you can reorganize files in Document Management just like any other document.

### Q: Is there a file size limit?
**A:** Files up to 500MB are supported per attachment.

## Tips for Best Results

### Use Clear File Names
Before attaching, rename files descriptively:
- Good: `Level-3-concrete-pour-2024-12-01.jpg`
- Not ideal: `IMG_4532.jpg`

### Select the Right Project
Make sure you've selected the correct project in the form before submitting. Files are linked to whichever project is selected.

### Check Your Uploads
After submitting, you can verify files appeared in Document Management under the Forms folder.

### Report Issues
If attachments don't appear after a few minutes, contact support with the form name and submission time.
