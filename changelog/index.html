    Deep Space Changelog¶

    The release history of the Deep Space platform — DS Pro, DS Sync, DS Site, KAI and the platform itself — from the first DS Sync for Revit release in October 2020 through to July 2025. 91 updates, hosted here permanently with their original text and the images that could be recovered.

    Where the changelog continues

    From August 2025 the product changelog is published on Deep Space Horizon at feedback.deepspacesync.com/changelog. This site is the permanent home of everything before that point, and of DS Pro releases published here since.

    Tip: filter by product

    Use the Search bar (or Ctrl+K) and type a category such as DS Platform, DS Pro for Construction or KAI.
