<section class="bg-capability-section" aria-labelledby="experience-title">
  <div class="bg-capability-section-heading"><p class="bg-eyebrow">03 / The experience behind the work</p><h2 id="experience-title">Grounded in<br>real projects.</h2></div>
  <div class="bg-capability-prose">
    <h3>Construction delivery</h3>
    <p>Luke's background spans infrastructure, healthcare, aviation and complex building projects. Experience includes BIM management and coordination at the New Royal Adelaide Hospital and Flinders Medical Centre, alongside digital advisory and implementation work on projects including Cross River Rail, Pitt Street Station and Christchurch's Parakiore Metro Sports Facility.</p>
    <h3>Implementation across teams and regions</h3>
    <p>As a former Revizto Implementation Services Director for ANZ and Asia Pacific, Luke worked across customer rollout, collaboration workflows and user adoption. That experience informs a practical approach to implementation: understand the project, work alongside its people and connect the software to the way the team delivers.</p>
    <h3>Building tools and sharing knowledge</h3>
    <p>A founder of Deep Space, Autodesk Expert Elite alumnus and author of <a href="https://wrw.is/">What Revit Wants</a> since 2008, Luke combines software development and automation with a long record of sharing practical technical knowledge. That depth supports both detailed problem-solving and the wider decisions needed to deliver change.</p>
