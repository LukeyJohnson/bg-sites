  <h2 id="release-title">Deep Space + Black Grid Media Release</h2>
  <div class="bg-media-release-body">
    <p><strong>For immediate release:</strong></p>
    <p>Deep Space continues to grow as an all-in-one dedicated SaaS platform purpose-built for midsize construction companies, with our focus firmly on delivering scalable, globally expanding solutions. To ensure we remain agile and innovative in driving product growth, we are evolving how we manage complex projects and services.</p>
    <p>To that end, our BIM and Enterprise Solutions division, DS Pro, has been spun out into a specialised partner company, Black Grid, led by a founder of Deep Space (Luke Johnson). Black Grid will act as both an implementation partner and a consulting arm, taking on advanced BIM, services contracts, and enterprise-scale projects. While it will operate independently, Black Grid will also contribute directly to the development of Deep Space intellectual property, ensuring seamless integration across both organizations.</p>
    <p>This structure allows the Deep Space core team to stay fully focused on our Horizon product roadmap while giving customers with more complex needs access to a highly capable partner in Black Grid. The result is a more streamlined, integrated ecosystem: Deep Space as your global SaaS AEC backbone, and Black Grid as your consulting and enterprise delivery partner.</p>
    <p>We’re confident this model will strengthen our ability to serve you by keeping Deep Space simple, focused, and powerful, while offering expanded services and expertise when you need them.</p>
