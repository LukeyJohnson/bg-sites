# Privacy and analytics

Updated 24 November 2026.

Black Grid uses Google Analytics to understand visits to this website, What Revit Wants, PMGrid, PulseGrid, ProXP and MyTendrils, and which public product, setup, download and checkout actions visitors use. The shared reporting property uses Brisbane time and Australian dollars. What Revit Wants also retains its existing historical reporting property.

Analytics uses a random browser identifier and first-party cookies shared across Black Grid subdomains. It measures page views, broad device/browser information, referrals and engagement. For signed-in apps, page labels remove project and portfolio identifiers and use a fixed product title. URL queries and fragments are removed from page and referrer information. Only recognised public campaign labels are accepted.

We do not send names, email addresses, account IDs, licence keys, checkout session references, project/task content, prompts or captured conversations to Analytics. Automatic form, site-search and outbound-link capture is disabled in the shared stream. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled. The ShadowGrid purchase count uses a one-way hashed reference after the payment service verifies a paid order; test/complimentary orders are excluded. It does not report revenue amounts or replace Stripe's financial records.

Your choice applies to this browser across the covered Black Grid subdomains. Turning analytics off also clears its first-party Analytics cookies. Browser **Global Privacy Control**, **Do Not Track**, and Google's Analytics opt-out setting are respected. A browser privacy signal takes precedence over the Allow button. Other browsers and devices have their own choices. Essential authentication, payment and licence services continue to work independently.

Google processes analytics information under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The shared property's user/event retention is recorded in the operating guide. For product-specific account, licence and service processing, see [ShadowGrid privacy](projects/shadowgrid/privacy.md) or the relevant application's privacy notice.
