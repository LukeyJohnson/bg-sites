ShadowGrid's cloud connection supports licence activation, device allowances, trial eligibility, payment fulfilment and framework updates. It processes opaque installation identifiers, licence/payment references, request information and device registrations needed for those services. Trial and activation rate limits use request IP information. Agreement records identify the version/hash and the time the service observed an acceptance assertion or verified Stripe consent; they do not claim to record when a person actually read every term.

Personal desktop tool files remain on the PC unless you deliberately upload them to the optional web marketplace. Framework licence and update requests do not automatically upload Revit models, private scripts or AI conversations. The guidance-only local MCP reads bundled rules offline and does not inspect models or send prompts to Black Grid. A script you run can access files and network services. Your selected AI provider receives the information you choose to send to it.

Black Grid uses Google Cloud for the canonical service and Stripe for payment processing. Card details are entered into Stripe Checkout; the ShadowGrid website and licence service do not collect card numbers. Purchase entitlement, payment references and agreement records are retained for fulfilment, recovery, refunds, security and business/legal records. Device/trial records support licence limits. Public documentation and release endpoints do not expose private licence records. Service providers may process information under their own policies and in locations beyond the selected Australian service region.

The desktop protects saved activation material using Windows user protection. Copies of agreement text are stored locally in the agreements folder. Keep your Windows account, keys and tool backups secure. Framework updates preserve personal files and activation state; a valid licence refusal does not delete your own scripts.

For access, correction, purchase recovery or privacy enquiries, contact Black Grid using the support address stated in your order/EULA or the contact details at https://blackgrid.com.au/capability/. Some records may need to be retained for legal or transaction requirements. Do not submit complete models or private tool libraries unless an agreed support engagement specifically needs them.

## Optional script marketplace

The marketplace stores the account identifier and verified email needed for sign-in and private invitations, plus the tool metadata, source files, guides, source-use licences, version hashes and review records you deliberately submit. It reuses Black Grid's suite identity with PulseGrid; this does not automatically share tools or company workspaces between products. Google Cloud hosts the marketplace API, a dedicated Australian database and private object storage; identity and service providers may process information in other locations.

A private invitation identifies one release and an intended verified recipient. Accepting binds the access grant to that account. The owner can revoke future downloads. The service cannot recall files already downloaded. Copy-link and email-draft actions let you send an invitation yourself; the service does not automatically email it.

Public submission makes a separate snapshot containing the disclosed source, guide, licence, public author name and release metadata. It is reviewed before public listing. Private recipient lists and the original private version history are excluded. Public licences can continue to apply to copies already distributed after a listing is withdrawn.

Version histories and review records are preserved to avoid silently replacing scripts. Contact Black Grid for access, correction, removal or rights requests; any required business/security retention will be explained for the request. Do not upload confidential client information, models or credentials. Website analytics does not receive private tool names, sources, invitations, recipient emails or account identifiers.
