    ShadowGrid End User Licence Agreement¶

    Version: SG-EULA-2026-11-13.1. Prepared 13 November 2026. This version applies when expressly presented and accepted with your order or activation.

A person accepting for an organisation must have authority to do so. If you do not agree, do not activate the software; contact us about any purchase already made. Your statutory rights remain available.

    Your order or beta invitation identifies the licence type, price, supported application, term and any specifically agreed additions. A separately signed agreement prevails over an inconsistent provision here. A general roadmap, demonstration or discussion does not add a feature or service commitment, but nothing here removes rights arising from a representation or promise that the law protects.

    2. What the product provides¶

    ShadowGrid is a licensed framework for running compatible customer-selected Python tools inside the supported host application. The initial product is ShadowGrid for full Autodesk Revit on Windows. Its release documentation specifies supported Revit years, tested builds and prerequisites. Revit LT cannot load the add-in. Your Autodesk licence, operating system and chosen AI services are separate.

    The v1 package includes the local execution framework, bundled runtime, script selector, installation/activation components, framework update mechanism, design guidance and the lightweight guidance MCP described in that release. The MCP provides rules about script structure and compatibility. It does not supply a script library, operate your model, or confer rights to marketplace tools.

    Personal files are ordinary editable Python files stored outside installer-owned files. Saving and rerunning the selected file permits rapid iteration without restarting Revit; imported modules, dependencies or framework changes may require a restart or additional setup.

    3. Founding paid licence¶

    Unless your order expressly states different terms, the founding offer is A$249 excluding applicable GST, paid once. The complete tax-inclusive total must be disclosed before payment. It grants:

    • A licence for one named individual, registered on up to two devices for that individual's work, including work for their employer or clients. This is not two user seats or a floating/team licence.
    • Perpetual local use of the purchased v1 release, together with covered v1 updates released during the included update period. “Perpetual” means the licence has no scheduled time expiry, subject to the termination provisions below; it does not mean ownership of the framework's source code or permanent cloud hosting.
    • 12 calendar months of update entitlement from purchase. The order and entitlement record identify the end date. An update must fall within that period and the purchased major version to be covered. No particular number of updates, new Revit year or future feature is promised unless expressly included in the order.

    When that update period ends, a covered release remains licensed for local use. We do not require a maintenance renewal merely to keep running it. You may decline an uncovered update. A future renewal or major-version upgrade is optional and separately priced; there is no automatic subscription or automatic renewal charge under this founding offer.

    Perpetual rights do not guarantee that an old release will remain compatible with future Windows, Autodesk, security policy, hardware or third-party changes. We will describe the supported environment accurately and honour applicable statutory obligations.

    4. Trial and beta licences¶

    A standard trial lasts 14 days from its initial issue, as recorded in its signed entitlement. A private beta licence lasts until the date in its invitation or entitlement, with any evaluation restrictions stated there. Neither is a perpetual paid licence, and neither converts automatically to a purchase or subscription.

    Trial/beta rights end at the recorded expiry unless we expressly extend them. Do not evade eligibility or expiry controls to obtain repeated evaluations. Pre-release builds may contain defects or incomplete features; use suitable test models and backups. Known limitations and the intended evaluation scope must be disclosed with the invitation. Reporting a defect does not transfer ownership of your models or scripts to us.

    A paid founding licence supplied while a build is labelled beta remains a paid licence with the rights in its order; the beta label does not silently turn that purchase into a time-limited evaluation. We must distinguish those offers at checkout and in the entitlement issued.

    5. Activation, offline use and updates¶

    Initial activation and device registration require the Black Grid licence service. Licence transfers, replacement-device registration, purchase recovery, new downloads and update checks can require connectivity and our assistance. Keep your purchase receipt and private activation key. Release an old installation before moving a device registration, or contact support if it is unavailable. A named-user change needs our agreement; we will reasonably assist a genuine business seat reassignment that does not create extra users.

    A valid activated paid perpetual entitlement allows offline local execution of its covered release without a recurring cloud lease. Trial, beta or subscription entitlements may have a signed expiry/offline deadline. A failed network request alone does not cancel valid cached perpetual rights. An authoritative refusal for a valid reason under this agreement can block subsequent execution; it does not delete your scripts or forcibly interrupt a running model operation.

    The framework checks signed update metadata and verifies the installer. Installation waits for Revit and the optional local MCP connection to close; you control saving and closing your work. Routine updates retain the Black Grid publisher identity rather than requiring a new trust certificate each time. Organisation policies can still require IT approval. Back up tools and settings normally.

    We may change infrastructure or retire a service. Where reasonably practicable we will provide advance notice and a reasonable activation-continuity or recovery path for paid covered releases, consistent with your perpetual rights and applicable law. The purchase does not include an unlimited hosted-service availability guarantee, a source-code escrow, or support for all future devices and software environments. An outage or retirement does not itself convert perpetual execution rights into a subscription.

    6. Your tools, AI and third-party components¶

    You retain the rights you already have in your scripts, models, data and other materials. We acquire no ownership merely because you create or run them with ShadowGrid. You may keep, read, export and share your own scripts subject to your rights and any third-party obligations. A recipient needs their own execution licence if they use the licensed ShadowGrid framework.

    Your AI provider's terms and any source material govern rights in AI output. Review generated tools for correctness and third-party rights. A ShadowGrid purchase does not certify an AI output or promise exclusive ownership of it. Your chosen AI is billed separately; running a saved local tool does not consume Black Grid AI tokens.

    Bundled open-source components retain their own licences and notices, supplied with the package. Their licences govern those components and this agreement does not remove rights they grant. Third-party scripts, marketplace tools and separately commissioned work have their own applicable terms. A marketplace purchase does not transfer ownership of the ShadowGrid framework.

    You may install and use the supplied framework within your licence, keep reasonable backup copies and author tools against its documented interface. Black Grid and its licensors retain rights in the proprietary framework, branding, installer, service and other supplied materials except where another licence applies.

    You must not share activation keys, falsify entitlements, bypass paid framework licensing, redistribute proprietary framework binaries/source without permission, or copy protected framework implementation into a redistributed product. Do not use another customer's credentials or access private libraries without authority. These restrictions apply only to the extent permitted by law; they do not prohibit activities or rights that the law makes non-excludable.

    Using AI to author your own tools is expressly permitted. We do not claim ownership of an idea, general development knowledge, the Revit API, or your independently created implementation merely because you learned how to use ShadowGrid. Contractual restrictions and technical protections are not a guarantee that the framework cannot be independently recreated.

    8. Professional use and responsibilities¶

    ShadowGrid executes ordinary code with access to the host session and the user's computer; it is not a sandbox. A tool can change a model, write files or communicate over a network. Select trusted tools, review their behaviour, maintain backups and test changes on an appropriate model copy. A design guide or syntax check does not verify every API call or establish engineering, architectural or regulatory compliance.

    You remain responsible for professional review, project approvals and using results appropriately. Those responsibilities do not excuse a defect in the framework or remove our statutory obligations. Do not provide private models, client scripts, credentials or personal data to an AI or support channel without appropriate authority.

    9. Support and future products¶

    The founding offer includes reasonable assistance with installation, activation, purchase recovery and reporting framework defects through the stated support channel. It does not include an agreed response-time SLA, bespoke script development, training, BIM certification or an ongoing consultancy engagement unless the order says so. We may separately quote those services.

    Company cloud libraries, a public catalogue, creator payments, a marketplace, hosted AI/token plans, cloud storage and integrations with Navisworks, Excel, Microsoft Project or other applications are possible future products. They are included only when explicitly supplied under your order or a separately accepted offer. We do not automatically opt you into a future paid service, upload a private library, or obtain marketplace distribution rights from you.

    We may set prices for future products and new purchases. We cannot retrospectively shorten your paid update period, remove covered perpetual execution rights or impose a mandatory recurring fee on those rights by updating a webpage.

    10. Refunds and statutory rights¶

    The founding offer adds a 14-day purchase refund policy: contact support within 14 days with your receipt and request a refund if the product is unsuitable. A short description helps us resolve problems but is not a waiver of rights. This voluntary policy is additional to statutory rights and does not impose a 14-day limit on a remedy provided by law.

    Nothing in this agreement excludes, restricts or modifies a consumer guarantee, remedy or other right that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified. Where Australian Consumer Law applies, applicable rights and remedies remain available, including for a qualifying failure discovered after the voluntary refund period.

    A full refund or payment reversal that validly cancels the purchase ends the corresponding licence; stop using that licensed framework and remove unauthorised copies. Your own scripts and models remain yours. A disputed charge is reviewed reasonably; a dispute is not permission to confiscate customer files, automatically forfeit unrelated purchases or remove non-excludable rights. A mistaken block can be challenged through support.

    11. Suspension and termination¶

    We may suspend the affected activation or service to address a credible security incident, fraudulent purchase, unauthorised key sharing or material licence breach. The response must be proportionate. We will give the reason and notice where reasonably practicable, permit a reasonable opportunity to resolve a remediable breach, and restore access when the reason is resolved. Immediate action is reserved for situations where reasonably necessary to prevent ongoing harm or unauthorised access.

    We may terminate for an unremedied material breach after written notice and at least 14 days to remedy it, or where a valid refund/cancellation ends the licence. We cannot terminate a paid perpetual licence merely because we stop selling the product, change strategy, introduce a subscription or receive good-faith criticism. You may stop using the software at any time; refund rights are governed by section 10 and applicable law.

    On valid termination, cease use of the terminated proprietary framework. We do not require deletion of your independently owned scripts, models, business records or materials covered by a separate continuing licence. Terms concerning accrued rights, ownership and lawful limitations continue where relevant.

    No provision limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, or liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or another matter that the applicable law prevents us from limiting. We do not promise that every customer-authored or third-party tool will be correct, suitable or uninterrupted; that does not remove guarantees applying to our own supply.

    Subject to those protections and only where legally permitted, our aggregate contractual liability for a claim concerning an affected licence is limited to the licence fees paid for it. This limitation does not override statutory remedies and does not apply to the extent that relying on it would be unfair or unlawful.

    Please first describe a dispute through support so we can reasonably attempt resolution. This does not prevent urgent court relief, a statutory complaint or seeking an available remedy. Applicable Australian law governs the agreement; no exclusive forum term here removes a customer's mandatory local rights. If a provision is invalid, the remaining provisions continue to the extent they can operate lawfully.

    Keep a copy of the accepted agreement and order. We retain the accepted version identifier and relevant acceptance/purchase records. Privacy handling is explained in the separately available ShadowGrid privacy notice.

    A new posted EULA applies to a new supply or expressly agreed variation; it does not silently rewrite an existing purchase. If a future product needs different terms, we will present them before the relevant purchase or activation. Updating a covered release alone does not require surrendering already granted perpetual rights. An express change must preserve rights that the law prevents us from taking away.
